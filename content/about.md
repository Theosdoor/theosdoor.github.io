There's more to me than transformers and interpretability. Here's some of what I care about outside of AI safety — the things that don't quite make the CV cut, but that I like to show anyway.

## Giving back

Some of the work I'm gladdest to have done is volunteering around County Durham.

- **A-Level Maths tutoring** — I've regularly tutored sixth-form students at local state schools, most recently a ten-week programme of weekly one-hour A-level Maths sessions at Durham Johnston Comprehensive School. I love finding the explanation that makes a hard idea finally click.
- **County Durham Foodbank** — warehouse support: sorting donations and packing food parcels.
- **Sherburn House Charity** — spending time with residents through conversation, games, and walks. Good company, mostly.
